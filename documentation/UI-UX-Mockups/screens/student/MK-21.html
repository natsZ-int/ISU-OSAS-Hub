How to read 'not applicable'A criterion the scholarship does not configure is reported as not applicable and excluded from the decision. It is not treated as a failure.

What this means

Eligibility is a screening result, not an award decision. Meeting every criterion means your application will be reviewed; it does not guarantee approval. The review also considers the documents you upload.

Design intent

Explain, criterion by criterion, why the student is or is not eligible for a particular scholarship, without creating an application.

What this screen establishes

  1. One row per criterion: GWA, family income, program, year level, enrollment status.
  2. Each row shows the student's value, the required value, the outcome and a reason sentence.
  3. Outcomes are met, not met, or not applicable; an unconfigured criterion is never a failure.
  4. The overall verdict is derived from the criteria, not computed separately.
