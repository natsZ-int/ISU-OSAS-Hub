A dash means the scholarship does not configure that criterion, so the factor is excluded from both the numerator and the denominator of the score.

Design intent

Expose every scoring factor behind each recommendation so the ranking is inspectable rather than opaque.

What this screen establishes

  1. Columns mirror the five factors: academic, financial, program, year level, documents.
  2. Each factor cell shows met or not met; document readiness shows a fraction.
  3. Total score is the weight-renormalised sum, displayed as a percentage.
  4. Factors that do not apply are shown as a dash and excluded from the denominator.

Structural source: WF-S-09 in the wireframe set. The mockup restates the same information architecture in the institutional palette, so a change to one is a change to both.
