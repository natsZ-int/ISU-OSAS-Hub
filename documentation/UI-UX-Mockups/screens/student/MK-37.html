What happens next

  1. You receive a request number immediately.
  2. The office assigns a handler and starts processing.
  3. You may be asked for additional information.
  4. The request is completed or cancelled, and you are notified.

Processing time

Typical

Design intent

Collect the service type, a description of at least ten characters, and the supporting documents required by that service.

What this screen establishes

  1. Service type is pre-selected when arriving from the catalogue.
  2. Description requires at least ten characters; the counter shows the remaining minimum.
  3. Attachments are limited to PDF, JPG, JPEG and PNG at 5 MB each.
  4. Required attachments are listed per the chosen service type's requirements.

Structural source: WF-S-17 in the wireframe set. The mockup restates the same information architecture in the institutional palette, so a change to one is a change to both.
