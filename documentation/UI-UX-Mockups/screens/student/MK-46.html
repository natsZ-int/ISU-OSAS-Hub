One interface, three widths

The same screen rendered at 1440, 1024 and 390 px. Nothing is redrawn for the smaller viewports: the sidebar leaves the flow, the top bar sheds its search field, grids collapse and tables restack. Each frame below is a live render at that width, not a picture of one.

DesktopPersistent 264 px sidebar; six summary tiles in two rows; two-column split for charts and backlogs.
TabletSidebar collapses to a menu toggle; tiles fall to two columns; split sections stack.
MobileSingle column; table rows restack with column labels; primary action stays full width.

Design intent

Give the student one screen that answers: what can I apply for, what is happening to my applications, and what is scheduled next.

What this screen establishes

  1. Four summary tiles: open scholarships, active applications, upcoming appointments, open requests.
  2. Enrollment banner appears only when verification is missing for the current term.
  3. Recommended scholarships are ranked by match score and capped at three cards.
  4. Upcoming appointments show date, time, counselor and status — never counselor notes.

Structural source: WF-S-01 in the wireframe set. The mockup restates the same information architecture in the institutional palette, so a change to one is a change to both.
