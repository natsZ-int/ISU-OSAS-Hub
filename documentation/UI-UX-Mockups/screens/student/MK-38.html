Office remarks

Remarks are written by the assigned handler and are visible to you. Internal handling notes are never shown on this screen.

Design intent

Show one request, its documents, its status history, and any information the office still needs from the student.

What this screen establishes

  1. An outstanding information request is surfaced as a prominent banner, not a hidden status.
  2. Office remarks and internal remarks are separated; only the former is shown here.
  3. Additional documents can be attached only while the request is non-terminal.
  4. Status history is append-only with actor and timestamp.

Structural source: WF-S-19 in the wireframe set. The mockup restates the same information architecture in the institutional palette, so a change to one is a change to both.
