Self-lockout guardYou cannot deactivate or demote your own account. Another administrator must make that change.

Danger zone

Deactivating blocks login immediately. Records owned by this account are retained.

Design intent

Create an account with an explicit role, or change an existing account's name, email, role and active state.

What this screen establishes

  1. Role is a required, explicit choice — it is never inferred from the email address.
  2. Email must be unique across all accounts; the field reports a duplicate inline.
  3. On create, an initial password is required and must be confirmed.
  4. On edit, the password field is left blank and only replaced when filled, so editing a role does not silently reset a password.

Structural source: WF-A-03 in the wireframe set. The mockup restates the same information architecture in the institutional palette, so a change to one is a change to both.
