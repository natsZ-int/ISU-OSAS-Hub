Matching weights

Weights must total 100. They determine ranking order only; they never change eligibility.

Active weights

35
GWA relative to the scholarship threshold.
25
Household income relative to the ceiling.
20
Binary: the program is eligible or it is not.
10
Binary: the year level is eligible or it is not.
10
Verified or uploaded documents over required documents.

Total

100.00
Valid
A total outside 100 ± 0.01 is refused with the exact difference shown, and nothing is saved.

How a score is formed

Each factor is scored 0–1, multiplied by its weight, then summed. The sum is divided by the weights that applied, so a skipped criterion does not depress the score. The result is multiplied by 100 and rounded to two decimals. (0.90×35 + 0.95×25 + 1×20 + 1×10 + 0.67×10) / 100 = 92.20
!
Weights are globalA single weight set applies to every scholarship and every student. Per-scholarship weights are not supported by the delivered schema.

Design intent

Set the five weights that drive recommendation ranking, with a hard requirement that they total 100.

What this screen establishes

  1. Five weights: academic, financial, program, year level and document readiness.
  2. The live total is displayed and must equal 100, with a tolerance of 0.01; otherwise saving is refused.
  3. Only one weight set is active at a time; saving supersedes the previous set.
  4. The change is attributed to the signed-in administrator and time-stamped.

Structural source: WF-A-11 in the wireframe set. The mockup restates the same information architecture in the institutional palette, so a change to one is a change to both.
